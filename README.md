Vice Cover Studio: a web app that turns your photos and a caption into GTA-style cover art — multi-panel poster, hero and grid layouts, a Vice City sunset or neon colour grade, halftone dots, vignette and film grain — and exports it as a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. It runs entirely offline from static files; your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Pick up to three photos (or use the built-in samples), choose a layout and grade, click a panel to crop and position it, type a caption, and hit Export PNG.

## Notes

- The display font is Archivo Black (SIL Open Font License 1.1), shipped as `app/fonts/ArchivoBlack-Regular.ttf`.
- The two sample photos under `app/samples/` were made for make.fun with an image model and are free to use.
- No Rockstar Games logo, artwork or font is used anywhere in this app.
